Our Simple 4-Step Process to Success.

We handle 100% of the technical setup. Your daily shipments and business operations continue without a single interruption.

01
Step 1 · Day 1–3

Discover: We learn about your business

We start with a friendly, non-technical 30-minute conversation. We walk through your daily routine: where paperwork piles up, where orders get delayed, and what drives your operations team crazy. You don’t need any technical vocabulary—just show us how you work today.

Key Deliverables: • Discovery questionnaire review • Time-drain identification • Initial feasibility check
02
Step 2 · Day 4–7

Design: We create a custom automation blueprint

We map out an architecture specifically tailored to Indian MSME operations. We select lightweight, highly reliable AI models and design workflows that plug directly into the tools your team already knows: WhatsApp, Gmail, Tally, and Excel. No heavy new software to learn.

Key Deliverables: • Custom Automation Blueprint • Visual before-and-after workflow diagram • Fixed-price implementation scope
03
Step 3 · Week 2–3

Deploy: We build and launch your AI workflows

Our team constructs the automation pipeline in our secure sandbox and tests it rigorously against real samples of your invoices, bills of lading, or buyer messages. Once verified, we deploy it with zero interruption to your ongoing shipments and day-to-day dispatches.

Key Deliverables: • End-to-end integration • Dual-validation safety guardrails • Live system testing on real trade files
04
Step 4 · Week 4 & Beyond

Dominate: We provide support and help you scale

Technology is only as good as the people using it. We conduct live, hands-on training sessions with your operations team and provide simple, 2-minute video references in plain English or Hindi. We stay by your side to ensure your operations run faster, cheaper, and error-free.

Key Deliverables: • Team training sessions • Simple bilingual documentation • 30-day post-launch optimization
